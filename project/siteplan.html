Site Name: Explore Sri Lanka

Discover. Explore. Plan your perfect trip to Sri Lanka.

One Island. Endless Experiences.

Discover golden beaches, ancient kingdoms, breathtaking landscapes, incredible wildlife, and unforgettable adventures across Sri Lanka.

Site Purpose

The purpose of Explore Sri Lanka is to help tourists discover the island's attractions and plan their trips with confidence. The scope covers travel planning for visitors to Sri Lanka, from choosing destinations to organising a journey and estimating its cost.

Services

  • Browse and filter tourist attractions by region and interest.
  • Build a personalised itinerary from selected attractions.
  • Estimate a travel budget for a planned trip.

Information

  • Descriptions and images of attractions, beaches, wildlife, and cultural sites.
  • Locations and regional guides to help with route planning.
  • Practical travel tips and estimated costs.

Target Audience

The target audience for Explore Sri Lanka includes international tourists, adventure travellers, families, and cultural enthusiasts who are interested in visiting Sri Lanka. Visitors use the website to learn about destinations, discover attractions, plan itineraries, and obtain practical travel information.

Scenario 1: International Tourist

A traveller from Canada is planning a two-week holiday to Sri Lanka and wants information about beaches, wildlife safaris, cultural sites, and recommended destinations. The visitor uses the website to create a travel itinerary and decide which attractions to visit.

Scenario 2: Adventure Traveller

An adventure traveller is looking for outdoor activities such as hiking, surfing, and wildlife experiences. The visitor uses the website to find information about Ella, Arugam Bay, Yala National Park, and other adventure destinations.

Scenario 3: Family

A family planning a vacation wants to find safe and family-friendly attractions. They use the website to discover beaches, national parks, cultural sites, and travel tips suitable for children.

Scenario 4: Cultural Enthusiast

A cultural enthusiast wants to learn about Sri Lanka's history and heritage. The visitor uses the website to explore information about Sigiriya, Kandy, Anuradhapura, and Polonnaruwa.

Description of Planned Content

Explore Sri Lanka is an interactive travel-planning website that allows users to discover tourist attractions, create personalised itineraries, and estimate travel budgets.

Wireframes

Reasons for the Site

Tourists often struggle to decide where to visit in Sri Lanka and how to organise their travel plans. This website provides attraction information and interactive tools to help users plan their journeys efficiently.

Color Scheme

This section will explain the color scheme used throughout the website, highlighting the primary colors and their significance in representing the essence of Sri Lanka.

  • Ocean Blue: Represents the beautiful coastal waters of Sri Lanka.
  • Jungle Green: Symbolizes the lush greenery and wildlife.
  • Sunset Gold: Reflects the stunning sunsets and cultural richness.

Interactive Tools

This section will feature interactive tools such as a trip planner, budget calculator, and map to help tourists organize their visit to Sri Lanka effectively.
